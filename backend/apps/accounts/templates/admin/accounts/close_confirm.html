Closing an account works like the app's own Delete account:

  • the person can never sign in again (all sessions are signed out);
  • their name, email, phone, PIN and devices are erased;
  • their listings, artisan profile, CV, jobs and rider status are taken down;
  • wallet, payment and delivery records are kept, anonymised, so your books still balance.

This can't be undone.
